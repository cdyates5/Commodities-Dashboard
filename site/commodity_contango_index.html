Methodology & data

This is an independent reconstruction of Variant Perception's Commodity Contango Index. The logic mirrors theirs; the data is rebuilt from freely available sources.

What it measures. For each commodity the annualized term-structure slope, (deferred − front) / front, scaled to %/yr. Positive is contango (deferred above front — an upward curve signalling current oversupply); negative is backwardation (front above deferred — current tightness). Averaging across commodities gives the index.

Why it leads. The signal is the capital–inventory cycle, not momentum. A glut today (high contango) depresses prices, producers cut capex, supply tightens, and prices rise roughly 12–18 months later — and the reverse for backwardation. So contango is read as a contrarian leading indicator of GSCI returns. The lead–lag panel confirms the empirical peak sits near 12–18 months (correlation ≈ +0.39), which is why the line is advanced 18 months to overlay VP's chart.

Historical line. The long history is the energy complex — WTI, natural gas, heating oil and RBOB gasoline — from EIA daily futures (front contract C1 vs the 4th contract C4, ~3 months apart, annualized). Energy is the majority of the GSCI and dominates roll dynamics, so it captures most of the index's shape. Each commodity is winsorized at ±60%/yr to tame the 2009 and 2020 blowouts, equal-weighted, then 3-month smoothed.

Extension to present. EIA discontinued these daily futures series in April 2024. From that seam the line is extended to today (shown dashed, with a gold marker) using the live futures strip: the front-month continuous contract versus the nearest still-listed deferred contract, annualised by time-to-maturity. The two methods overlap for fifteen months (Jan 2023–Apr 2024), and the live-strip reconstruction tracks the EIA series closely there — correlation +0.93 for WTI, +0.95 heating oil, +0.80 RBOB — so the extension is calibrated to the seam (a small level shift) and spliced on. A broad live cross-section of 21 commodities (energy, metals, agriculture, livestock) is shown separately below as a present-day sanity check on the energy line.

Data sources & honest limitations

Sources. EIA daily futures (Cushing WTI, Henry Hub, NY heating oil & RBOB term structure) for the 1990–2024 energy contango; S&P GSCI (^SPGSCI) for the year-over-year line, full history to present; live futures strips per commodity for the current cross-section. The accompanying Python script reproduces all of it.

Limitations. (1) The historical line is energy-only, not VP's full diversified basket — the shape matches but the magnitude in %/yr is larger than a metals-and-ags-diluted index would read; an optional z-score overlay removes the level difference. (2) The post-April-2024 extension uses the live strip, whose effective horizon drifts (the nearest still-listed deferred sits farther out earlier in the window and nearer today), and natural gas’s seasonal curve makes its live-strip fit weaker than oil’s (overlap correlation +0.42 vs +0.93); both the extension and the EIA line are winsorised at ±60%/yr and 3-month smoothed to keep them comparable. (3) The broad cross-section uses a constant-maturity interpolation reliable only while the relevant contracts are live, so it is a present-day snapshot rather than a backtest. (4) Front/deferred spacing differs slightly by commodity. None of this is investment advice.
