Method

Positioning: uses the CFTC Legacy report’s non-commercial category (large speculators). For each weekly report, net length = non-commercial longs − shorts; the group reading sums net length across constituents and divides by summed total open interest, so it is open-interest-weighted by construction.

Price index: constituents weighted by full-sample average dollar open interest (avg contracts × avg price × contract size), each rebased to 100 at the start of its available history (2000–01). Crude carries the energy index; gold carries metals — as in production-weighted benchmarks.
